Gentoo Penguins

Gentoo penguins can be recognised by their bright orange-red bill and orange feet.

They are one of the larger penguin species measuring around 75-90 cm tall.

  • Their habitats are cold sub-Antarctic islands, such as south Georgia, the Falkland Islands, and islands near Antarctica.
  • They nest on beaches and rocky ground. Both parent take turns keeping the egg warm and finding food.
  • They mainly eat krill, fish, and squid. They catch food while swimming and diving in the ocean.
  • Gentoo penguins are very fast swimmers and can reach about 36 km/h under water.
